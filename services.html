What we do

Three disciplines, one team, start to finish.

Applied research, independent technical advice, and custom software engineering — carried by the same people from first prototype through to production support.

R&D on ICT

Applied research into emerging information and communication technology — the exploratory work that de-risks what gets built next. We test an idea's technical feasibility before committing a full engineering budget to it.

  • A written feasibility assessment with clear go / no-go findings
  • Working prototypes used to test assumptions, not polished products
  • An evaluation of relevant emerging technology against your constraints
  • A recommendation on whether and how to proceed to a build

Typical engagement

  • 1Scope the questionDefine exactly what needs to be proven or disproven, and by when.
  • 2Build a narrow prototypeJust enough to test the riskiest assumption first.
  • 3Report findingsA clear written summary of what we learned and what it means for you.

Computer Consultancy

Independent technical advice on architecture, tooling, and technology strategy — grounded in what will actually run and scale, not in what's trending. We have no reseller relationships or licensing incentives to protect.

  • A systems or architecture review with prioritized findings
  • A technology strategy aligned to your team's actual capacity
  • Independent vendor and tooling assessments
  • A prioritized action plan your own team can execute

Typical engagement

  • 1AuditReview current architecture, tooling, and decision history.
  • 2AssessWeigh options against your real constraints — team size, budget, timeline.
  • 3RecommendDeliver a prioritized, actionable plan — not a generic best-practices deck.

Computer Programming

Custom software engineering and systems integration — designed, built, and supported by the team that scoped it. No hand-off between the people who understand the requirements and the people who write the code.

  • Custom application development, from scratch or on existing systems
  • Systems integration across existing tools and services
  • Code delivered in small, reviewable increments — not one big reveal
  • Ongoing engineering support after launch

Typical engagement

  • 1DesignArchitecture and delivery plan sized to the actual problem.
  • 2BuildShip in small increments with regular check-ins along the way.
  • 3SupportStay attached after launch for monitoring, fixes, and iteration.
