Engagement model

From initial diagnosis to ongoing support.

The same four-stage process runs whether the engagement is a two-week research spike or a multi-month build.

01

Discover

We start with the problem, not the solution: constraints, stakeholders, and what "done" actually looks like.

02

Design

An architecture and delivery plan sized to the real problem, reviewed with you before any engineering begins.

03

Build

Delivery in small, reviewable increments, with regular check-ins rather than a single reveal at the end.

04

Support

We remain engaged after launch for monitoring, fixes, and iteration. The team that built the system maintains it.

About Mpire

A small, senior team spanning research, advisory, and software delivery.
