About Mpire

A small, senior team working across research, advice, and software.

Mpire Global Technologies Sdn. Bhd. is a Kuala Lumpur based ICT company. We don't separate the people who study a problem from the people who build the fix — the same engineers carry it end to end.

Our story

Based in Kuala Lumpur, built for how software actually gets used.

Mpire Global Technologies Sdn. Bhd. is registered to carry out research and development in information and communication technology, computer consultancy, and computer programming activities.

We work as a small, senior team by design — close enough to a problem to research it properly, and hands-on enough to build and maintain what comes out of that research. There is no hand-off between the people who scope a project and the people who ship it.

That structure shapes what we take on: work where understanding the problem correctly matters as much as writing the code, and where someone needs to still be reachable after launch.

What we value

How we choose to work.

A handful of working principles that shape which projects we take and how we run them.

Evidence before opinions

Recommendations are grounded in prototypes, benchmarks, or a working proof of concept — not just familiarity with a tool.

No hand-offs to strangers

The engineers who scope a project are the ones who build, ship, and support it. Continuity of context is the point.

Small scope, sized honestly

We plan against the real problem rather than a generic template, and say plainly when something is bigger than it looks.

Process

From first conversation to ongoing support.

The same four-stage process runs whether the engagement is a two-week research spike or a multi-month build.

  • Stage 01

    Discover

    We start with the problem, not the solution — understanding constraints, stakeholders, and what "done" actually looks like before proposing anything.

  • Stage 02

    Design

    An architecture and delivery plan sized to the real problem, reviewed with you before any significant build work starts.

  • Stage 03

    Build

    Engineering happens in small, reviewable increments, with regular check-ins rather than a single reveal at the end.

  • Stage 04

    Support

    We stay attached after launch for monitoring, fixes, and iteration — the team that built it is the team that maintains it.
