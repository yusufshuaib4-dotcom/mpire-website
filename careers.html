Careers

We hire rarely, but well.

We are a small, senior team by design, not by circumstance. That means we are not always hiring, and when we are, we are looking for a specific kind of person rather than filling a headcount target.

How we think about hiring

Depth over headcount.

Every person at Mpire carries an engagement from diagnosis through delivery. That model only works with people who already have the judgment to work independently, which is why we hire slowly and stay small on purpose.

01

Judgment over credentials

We care more about how someone reasons through an ambiguous problem than which institutions or companies appear on their background.

02

Comfortable saying no

Independent advice sometimes means telling a client something they did not want to hear. We look for people who can do that constructively.

03

Ownership, start to finish

We do not separate the people who scope a problem from the people who solve it. Anyone who joins us should want that full arc of responsibility.

Current openings

Nothing formally open right now.

We keep a shortlist rather than a job board.

We do not currently have a formal vacancy posted, and we would rather say that plainly than publish a generic listing. When we do have a specific need, it is usually narrow: a particular kind of engineering background, or direct experience in one of the sectors we serve.

If you think you would be a strong fit for how we work, the most useful thing you can send us is a short note describing a problem you solved that you are proud of, and why. We read every message personally and keep genuinely strong candidates on file for when a fitting opening comes up.
