Representative engagements

What an engagement with Mpire typically looks like.

Client names and identifying details are withheld under standing confidentiality agreements. What follows describes the situation, our approach, and the outcome in terms general enough to protect that confidentiality while still being specific about how we work.

R&D on ICT

Testing a computer vision concept before committing to a full build

Situation
A prospective client needed to know whether a vision-based monitoring concept was technically viable before allocating a full engineering budget to it.
Approach
We built a narrow prototype against the riskiest assumption first, establishing a credible performance baseline within a short, fixed research window rather than a full production build.
Outcome
A written feasibility assessment gave the client a clear basis for deciding whether, and how, to proceed to a full build, well before committing significant capital.
Computer Consultancy

An independent architecture review ahead of a platform migration

Situation
An organisation planning a platform migration wanted an outside, vendor-neutral opinion on its proposed architecture before committing engineering time to it.
Approach
We reviewed the existing systems and the proposed design, tested the assumptions behind the key decisions, and weighed the options against the team's actual size and delivery capacity.
Outcome
A prioritized, actionable plan replaced a generic best-practices document, giving the internal team a sequence of changes it could execute with confidence.
Computer Programming

Building and supporting a custom system with no hand-off after launch

Situation
A client needed a custom application built and integrated with existing tools, with no separate hand-off to a support function once it shipped.
Approach
The same engineers who scoped the requirements carried the build through design, delivery in small increments, and post-launch support.
Outcome
The system shipped in reviewable stages with regular check-ins, and stayed supported by the people who built it rather than a separate desk.
Cross-discipline

Combining research and delivery for an early-stage technical bet

Situation
A founder needed to validate a technical approach quickly, then move straight into a working version if the underlying research held up.
Approach
Research and delivery ran under a single point of accountability: the same team that tested the underlying assumption then built the first working version.
Outcome
The client avoided the delay and cost of separately re-briefing a delivery team once the research concluded.
