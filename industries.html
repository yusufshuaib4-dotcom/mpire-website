Where we work

Sectors where research, advisory, and delivery meet real constraints.

Our three disciplines apply across sectors, but every sector brings its own regulatory pressure, legacy footprint, and risk tolerance. Here is how that typically shapes an engagement.

Capability, not case count

What we bring to each sector.

We are a small, senior firm. What follows describes the capabilities we apply in each sector and the considerations that typically shape the work there, not a roster of past clients.

Descriptions below are capability-oriented. Where we reference past work, see the confidentiality terms on our Work page.

01

Financial services and fintech

Advisory, R&D, programming

Financial services organisations tend to carry substantial legacy infrastructure alongside a genuine mandate to modernise, under a compliance regime that punishes moving too fast far more than moving too slowly. Our work here typically starts with an independent architecture or model-risk review before any build begins, so that the eventual delivery plan reflects the institution's actual regulatory and operational constraints rather than a generic technology roadmap.

Architecture review Model risk and validation Legacy system integration

02

Government and public sector

Advisory, programming

Public-sector technology decisions carry a duty of transparency and accountability that private-sector procurement rarely faces to the same degree. We work as an independent, vendor-neutral advisor in these engagements, documenting our reasoning at each stage so the sponsoring agency can defend the decision publicly, and building systems designed to be maintained by successor teams rather than dependent on the original vendor.

Vendor-neutral advisory Procurement support Maintainable system design

03

Logistics and supply chain

R&D, programming

Logistics operations run on tight margins and continuous uptime requirements, which means a new system has to prove itself against real operational data before it can be trusted at scale. We typically prototype against a representative slice of historical operations data first, establishing a measurable improvement over the existing process before recommending a full rollout.

Forecasting and optimisation Computer vision for operations Systems integration

04

Retail and consumer technology

Advisory, programming

Consumer-facing technology has to withstand real usage patterns from the first day it ships, with limited tolerance for a slow ramp-up period. We favour an incremental delivery approach here, shipping a narrow but complete version of a product early and expanding it against real usage rather than attempting a single large launch.

Product engineering Personalisation systems Platform migration

05

Manufacturing and industrial IoT

R&D, advisory, programming

Manufacturing environments combine physical equipment, sensor data, and operational technology that was rarely designed with modern software integration in mind. We spend meaningful time on-site or with detailed process documentation before proposing an approach, because the constraint that matters most in these engagements is usually the existing equipment and workflow, not the model or algorithm.

Sensor and IoT integration Computer vision for quality control Predictive maintenance
